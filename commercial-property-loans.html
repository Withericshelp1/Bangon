Commercial Property Finance That Goes Beyond the Banks

Securing commercial property finance is more complex than a standard home loan. Lenders assess the property type, lease agreements, business income and a range of other factors. The right broker can make the difference between a straight rejection and a tailored approval.

At Bang On Investments, we have access to a panel of specialist commercial lenders that go well beyond what the major banks can offer. We work with business owners and investors across Sydney including Chatswood, Strathfield, North Sydney, Concord and Burwood, as well as medical and healthcare professionals across the city who are setting up or expanding their practices. We also work with clients in the Southern Highlands looking to finance commercial premises in Bowral and Moss Vale.

Sydney's commercial property market spans everything from small professional suites and retail shopfronts to large industrial warehouses and purpose-built medical centres. Each property type has different lending characteristics and lender appetite. We know which lenders suit which property types and handle the complexity on your behalf.

Commercial Property Loan FAQs

A commercial property loan is secured against a commercial property asset such as an office, warehouse or retail premises. A business loan may be unsecured or secured against other assets. The right choice depends on your purpose and what security you have available. We will assess your situation and recommend the most suitable structure.

Most commercial lenders require a 20 to 35% deposit, though this varies by property type, location and the lender. In Sydney, property values and lease profiles also influence lender appetite. In some cases, additional residential security can reduce the deposit required.
