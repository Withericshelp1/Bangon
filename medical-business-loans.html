Specialist Business Finance for Healthcare Professionals

Setting up or expanding a medical practice requires a different approach to standard business lending. Lenders need to understand the regulatory environment, the lease or premises structure, typical equipment costs and the income profile of a healthcare business. Most general business lenders don't have this knowledge — which means many medical professionals are either declined or offered unsuitable products.

At Bang On Investments, we have a unique background in both medicine and finance. We've arranged business finance for GPs opening their first practices, specialists expanding their clinics, dentists fitting out new surgeries and allied health professionals growing their businesses. We know this space — and we know the lenders who do too.
