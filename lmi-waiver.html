What Is an LMI Waiver — and Why Does It Matter?

Lenders Mortgage Insurance (LMI) is a cost charged by lenders when you borrow more than 80% of a property's value. It's designed to protect the lender — not you — and can add $15,000 to $40,000 or more to the cost of buying a home, depending on your loan size and LVR.

For most buyers, avoiding LMI means saving a 20% deposit — which in Sydney's property market can take years. But if you're a medical professional, you may be able to borrow up to 90–95% of the property value without paying LMI at all. This is one of the most valuable financial benefits available to doctors, dentists, nurses and other healthcare workers — and many are unaware it exists.

Who Qualifies for an LMI Waiver?

LMI waiver eligibility varies by lender, but typically includes the following registered professionals:

  • General Practitioners (GPs)
  • Medical Specialists
  • Hospital-Employed Doctors
  • Dentists and Dental Specialists
  • Pharmacists
  • Optometrists
  • Nurses and Allied Health Professionals (lender dependent)
  • Interns and Residents (select lenders)
Eligibility also depends on your income, registration status, loan amount and LVR. We'll confirm your eligibility quickly across our panel of specialist lenders.

What Are the Conditions of an LMI Waiver?

LMI waivers for medical professionals are subject to lender-specific conditions, which typically include:

  • Borrowing up to 90% LVR (some lenders extend to 95%)
  • Purchasing a standard residential property
  • Meeting minimum income requirements
  • Being currently registered with the relevant professional body

Conditions vary between lenders — and the best available product for one profession may not be the best for another. This is where working with a specialist broker makes a real difference. We know the policies of each lender on our panel and will match you to the right one.
