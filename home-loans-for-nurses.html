A Home Loan That Understands Nursing Income

Nursing income can look complicated on paper. Shift allowances, overtime, casual loading, penalty rates and multiple employers can make standard lender assessments frustrating. Many nurses are surprised to find their borrowing capacity is lower than expected, simply because a lender does not know how to assess their income correctly.

At Bang On Investments, we work with lenders who understand how nurses earn and who are willing to include allowances and overtime in their income assessment. This can make a meaningful difference to your borrowing capacity and the home loan you can access.

Shift allowances and overtime can make up 20 to 40% of a nurse's total income in many roles. When a lender ignores this income, your borrowing capacity can be significantly understated. We make sure every dollar you earn is counted where possible.

We help nurses across NSW find the right home loan including across Greater Sydney in areas such as Chatswood, North Sydney, Strathfield, Concord and Willoughby, as well as the Southern Highlands where many healthcare workers are making the tree-change to Bowral and surrounds.
