Frequently Asked Questions

How is my vehicle loan repayment calculated?
Your repayment is calculated using the standard amortisation formula, based on your loan amount (minus any balloon payment), the interest rate and the loan term. The figure shown is an estimate — your actual repayment may differ slightly depending on how your lender calculates interest (daily or monthly) and any applicable fees.
What is a balloon payment or residual?
A balloon payment (also called a residual) is a lump sum due at the end of your loan term. Including one lowers your regular repayments but means a larger amount is owed at maturity — paid in cash, by refinancing or from the proceeds of selling the vehicle. Balloon payments are common in business vehicle finance structures such as chattel mortgage.
What interest rate should I enter?
Use an indicative rate as a starting point. Personal vehicle loan rates typically range from around 6% to 15%+ depending on your credit profile, the vehicle's age and the lender. Business vehicle finance (chattel mortgage, novated lease) may attract different rates. Contact us for a personalised rate estimate before committing.
Can I get pre-approved before I choose a vehicle?
Yes. We can arrange a pre-approval so you know your borrowing capacity before you start shopping. This puts you in a stronger negotiating position with dealers and private sellers — and speeds up the settlement process once you've found the right vehicle.
How do I get the best vehicle loan rate?
Rates are generally lowest for newer vehicles, borrowers with strong credit profiles and shorter loan terms. We compare options across 25+ lenders to find the most competitive rate available for your specific situation — often significantly better than going direct to a bank or accepting dealership finance.
