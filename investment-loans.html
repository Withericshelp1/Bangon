How We Structure Investment Loans

Stand-Alone Loan Structuring

For clients with a growing portfolio, we structure each investment property as a stand-alone security where possible. This reduces cross-collateralisation risk and protects your flexibility to sell or refinance individual assets without affecting the rest of your portfolio.

Want to know how this could work for you? Call 0414 406 887

Using Equity to Buy Your Next Property

One of the most powerful strategies we help clients with is using existing equity in their home or current investment properties as a deposit for their next purchase, without needing additional cash savings. We will assess your usable equity and show you exactly what is possible.

Find out how much equity you can access. Call 0414 406 887

Interest Only vs Principal and Interest

Choosing between interest-only and principal and interest repayments on an investment loan can significantly affect your cash flow and tax outcome. We explain both options clearly and help you choose the structure that suits your strategy, whether you are focused on growth, yield or both.

Not sure which is right for you? Call 0414 406 887

Tax-Effective Loan Structuring

We work alongside your accountant or financial adviser to structure your investment loan in a way that complements your tax strategy. This includes choosing the right loan type, entity structure and split between deductible and non-deductible debt.
