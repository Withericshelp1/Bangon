Is Your Home Loan Still Working for You?

The home loan market moves constantly and the rate you locked in two or three years ago may no longer be competitive. Refinancing is one of the simplest ways to reduce your repayments, access equity or switch to a loan structure that better suits where you are now.

At Bang On Investments, we make refinancing straightforward. We compare options from over 25 lenders including major banks, non-bank lenders and specialist providers, and handle the process from assessment to settlement. We work with homeowners and investors across Sydney including Chatswood, Strathfield, North Sydney, Concord, Willoughby, Pymble, Killara, Bowral and the Southern Highlands.

With Sydney median house prices remaining well above $1M in most suburbs, even a small rate reduction can represent a significant annual saving. On a $900,000 loan, a 0.5% rate reduction saves approximately $4,500 per year. We run the numbers for your specific situation at no cost.

Your Options

Reasons Our Clients Refinance

To Get a Lower Interest Rate

Even a 0.5% reduction in your interest rate can save tens of thousands of dollars over the life of your loan. On a $900,000 Sydney home loan that is around $4,500 per year back in your pocket. We compare the current market against your existing rate and show you exactly what a switch could save.

Find out if you are overpaying. Call 0414 406 887

To Access Equity

If your property has increased in value, refinancing can unlock equity for renovations, an investment property deposit, a vehicle purchase or other purposes. We will help you understand how much equity you can access and structure it correctly.

Want to know how much equity you can access? Call 0414 406 887

To Consolidate Debt

Rolling higher-interest debts such as credit cards and personal loans into your home loan can reduce your overall monthly repayments. We will help you assess whether debt consolidation makes sense for your situation and structure it appropriately.

Talk to us about debt consolidation. Call 0414 406 887

To Change Loan Structure

Moving from a fixed rate to variable, splitting your loan, switching from interest-only to principal and interest, or adding an offset account. Refinancing gives you the opportunity to reset your loan structure to better suit your current needs.
