Home Loans for Medical Professionals

Despite the long shifts, the doubles and the demands of your career, we know medical professionals need a comfortable place to call home, and deserve to get there sooner.

We draw on our deep understanding of both the medical and financial fields to design customised home loans for medical professionals. Ask us about our specialist Medico packages, within which Lenders Mortgage Insurance (LMI) can be waived at up to 90% LVR, meaning you can buy with a smaller deposit and avoid paying LMI altogether.

Eligible professionals include doctors, dentists, nurses, pharmacists and other registered healthcare practitioners. Eligibility depends on your registration, income and lender policy. Speak to us to check your situation.
