Chatswood Mortgage FAQ

What are typical property prices in Chatswood?
Chatswood's median house price sits around $3.5 million, reflecting its premium position on Sydney's Upper North Shore. Apartments — particularly those in newer developments near the station — typically range from $900,000 to $2M+ depending on size and aspect. Loan structures for high-value property require careful thought, and getting the right lender match is critical. We work with lenders experienced in this price bracket.
Is Chatswood a good area for property investment?
Chatswood offers strong investment fundamentals. The suburb benefits from excellent transport infrastructure (direct Metro access to the CBD and wider network), a diverse population, a major retail and commercial hub, and ongoing residential development. Rental demand is consistently strong, particularly for apartments near the station. As with any Sydney market, the right loan structure — including interest-only options and optimal LVR — makes a significant difference to investment returns.
Can you help with high-value home loans in Chatswood?
Yes. Loans above $2M or $3M are assessed differently by lenders — serviceability calculations, LVR limits and security requirements all change at higher price points. We regularly arrange finance for high-value properties across the North Shore and have established relationships with lenders experienced in this segment, including private banks and specialist high-LVR products.
Do you help first home buyers in Chatswood?
Chatswood's price point puts houses out of reach for many first home buyers, but apartments in the area are more accessible — and there are first home buyer stamp duty concessions available on purchases below certain thresholds. We work with first home buyers to maximise borrowing capacity, identify applicable government schemes and find lenders with the lowest upfront costs. It's worth having a conversation about what's possible before ruling anything out.
How quickly can you arrange a pre-approval for a Chatswood property?
We aim to turn around fully assessed pre-approvals within 2–5 business days in most cases, depending on lender and the complexity of your situation. The North Shore auction market moves fast — having a pre-approval in place before you begin inspecting is strongly recommended. Contact us early so we can prepare everything before you need it.
