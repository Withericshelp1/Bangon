Lenders Mortgage Insurance (LMI) is a cost charged by lenders when you borrow more than 80% of a property's value. It protects the lender — not you — and can cost between $15,000 and $40,000+ depending on the loan size. An LMI waiver means you avoid this cost entirely, which is a significant saving for doctors buying with a smaller deposit.
