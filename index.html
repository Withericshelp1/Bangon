Know the Difference

Fully Assessed Pre-Approval vs
Non-Fully Assessed Pre-Approval

Fully Assessed Pre-Approval

  • Reviewed and approved by a credit assessor
  • Your documents are verified upfront
  • A conditional approval is formally issued
  • Carries real weight with agents and vendors
  • Far less likely to fall over at full assessment
  • Recommended before attending auctions

Non-Fully Assessed Pre-Approval

  • System-generated estimate only
  • No document verification is completed
  • Can fall over when the real assessment begins
  • May not be accepted by real estate agents
  • Faster to obtain but significantly less reliable
  • Not recommended for auction purchases

Frequently Asked Questions

A mortgage broker acts as the middleman between you and the lenders. Rather than going directly to one bank, your broker compares options across a panel of lenders to find the best rate, structure and product for your situation. At Bang On Investments, we handle all the paperwork, negotiate on your behalf, and guide you from application through to settlement.

The minimum deposit is typically 5% of the purchase price, though a 20% deposit avoids Lenders Mortgage Insurance (LMI). For medical professionals, we can often access LMI waivers at up to 90-95% LVR. First home buyers may also be eligible for government schemes that reduce the deposit requirement significantly. We'll walk you through all available options in your first conversation.

A fully assessed pre-approval typically takes 3-5 business days once we have all required documents. A system-generated (non-fully assessed) pre-approval can be issued within hours, but we strongly recommend a fully assessed pre-approval if you're actively searching for property or planning to attend auctions.

In most cases, our service is completely free to you. We are paid a commission by the lender when your loan settles. This commission does not affect the rate or fees you pay. We're required by law to act in your best interests at all times, and we're happy to fully disclose how we're paid.

We have access to 25+ lenders on our panel, including major banks, second-tier lenders and specialist lenders. This gives us the flexibility to find competitive rates for a wide range of client situations, including complex income structures, self-employed borrowers and medical professionals.
