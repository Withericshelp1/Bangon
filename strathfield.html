Strathfield Mortgage FAQ

What are typical property prices in Strathfield?
Strathfield's median house price sits in the $3.5–4.5M range, placing it among Sydney's most expensive family suburbs. The premium is driven by the combination of grand Federation-era homes, large blocks, a prestigious school precinct and excellent transport links. Lending at this price point requires specialist experience — particularly around serviceability calculations, LVR limits and valuation support for prestige properties.
Which schools make Strathfield so desirable?
Strathfield's school precinct is one of the best in Sydney. Private schools in the area include Trinity Grammar School, Santa Sabina College, Meriden School and Strathfield College. The suburb also falls within the catchments for Strathfield Girls High School and Homebush Boys High School. This school premium is a key driver of both the high property values and the strong long-term capital growth the suburb has delivered.
Is Strathfield a good area for property investment?
Strathfield has delivered strong long-term capital growth, underpinned by genuine scarcity — the suburb is largely built out, with limited new supply. Rental demand is driven by families wanting school catchment access and professionals commuting via Strathfield Station. Gross yields are lower than outer-suburban markets, but capital growth has historically compensated. We can help structure investment loans that work for Strathfield's price point and rental dynamics.
Can you arrange a pre-approval before I start attending Strathfield auctions?
Yes — and we strongly recommend it. Strathfield auctions are competitive, with multiple registered bidders common and properties frequently selling well above reserve. A fully assessed pre-approval (not just an online indicative) lets you bid unconditionally and gives you a clear ceiling. We aim to turn these around within 2–5 business days. Contact us well before your first inspection.
Do you help with loans above $3M or $4M in Strathfield?
Yes. Jumbo loans above $3M are assessed differently by lenders — serviceability calculations change, LVR limits tighten and security requirements may vary. We regularly arrange high-value finance for Strathfield properties and have established relationships with private banks and specialist lenders experienced at this price point. If your situation is complex — self-employed income, multiple properties, trust structures — we can navigate that too.
